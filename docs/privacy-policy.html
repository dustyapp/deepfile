Deep File

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. About this policy

Deep File is an independent app for exploring historical and declassified material. This policy describes information used by the app and our support service. No Deep File account or email address is required to browse the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Reading progress, saved cases, reading history, preferences, and audio playback positions are stored locally on your device. Your saved reading and playback positions are not uploaded to our listening service. Listening requests themselves are processed by the services described below.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes iOS payments. Deep File uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, manage entitlements, and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat processes purchase and transaction records, subscription status, app/device technical information, and an automatically generated app user identifier. When you use listening, that identifier is linked to your anonymous listening profile so our server can verify access. We do not send your name, email address, or advertising identifier to RevenueCat, and we do not receive payment card details.

4. Online content and external services

Full-chapter audio is streamed from private Supabase storage and requires internet access and a valid Deep Dive purchase. When you first connect to listening, Supabase creates an anonymous profile without requesting a name, email, or password. Its identifier and session credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Supabase processes that identifier, authentication events, IP address, requested case/chapter, request times, and technical/security logs to authenticate you, verify access, deliver audio, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service issues. These requests can be associated with your listening profile. Cover images are loaded from external hosting, which receives your IP address and request information. Source links open external websites with their own privacy practices. Narration was created from our editorial scripts using Inworld; the app does not send your listening activity or microphone recordings to Inworld.

5. Support messages

If you choose to contact support, FormSubmit processes the contact details and message you enter and delivers them to our support inbox. We use that information to respond to your request. Do not include payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive information in support messages.

6. Advertising and tracking

Deep File does not include advertising SDKs or use purchase information to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for targeted advertising. RevenueCat purchase analytics help us understand purchases and subscription performance.

7. Retention and your choices

Use Delete listening profile in the app’s Privacy & Data screen to remove the anonymous Supabase profile, request deletion of its RevenueCat customer data, and clear local audio positions. RevenueCat processes customer deletion asynchronously. Technical/security logs may remain for the providers’ limited retention periods. Reading bookmarks remain local. Connecting to listening again creates a new anonymous profile; Restore Purchases can reconnect eligible Apple purchases. Deletion does not cancel subscriptions or erase Apple transaction records. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Support correspondence and records required by law may be retained. Use our private support form for access or deletion requests concerning other information.

8. Children

Deep File is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our support service, contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form on the Deep File support page.
