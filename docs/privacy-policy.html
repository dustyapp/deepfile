Deep File

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

1. About this policy

Deep File is an independent app for exploring historical and declassified material. This policy describes information used by the app and our support service. No Deep File account or email address is required to browse the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Reading progress, saved cases, reading history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Deep File does not upload these records to a developer-operated account service.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes iOS payments. Deep File uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, manage entitlements, and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat processes purchase and transaction records, subscription status, and an automatically generated anonymous app user identifier. Deep File does not send your name, email address, or advertising identifier to RevenueCat. We do not receive your payment card details.

4. Online content and external services

The app loads some images, documents, and document-viewer resources from external hosting services. These requests necessarily disclose your IP address and request information to the host. Links to external sources open services with their own privacy practices. Apple, RevenueCat, and external content providers process information under their respective privacy policies.

5. Support messages

If you choose to contact support, FormSubmit processes the contact details and message you enter and delivers them to our support inbox. We use that information to respond to your request. Do not include payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive information in support messages.

6. Advertising and tracking

Deep File does not include advertising SDKs or use purchase information to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for targeted advertising. RevenueCat purchase analytics help us understand purchases and subscription performance.

7. Retention and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until removed. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete Apple transaction records. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Purchase-service records and support correspondence may be retained for access restoration, support, security, and applicable legal obligations. Contact support to request access to or deletion of information we hold; records that must be retained may be excluded. Do not post privacy requests in public App Store reviews.

8. Children

Deep File is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our support service, contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form on the Deep File support page.
