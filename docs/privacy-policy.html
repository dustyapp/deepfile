Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Deep File ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Deep File is designed to collect as little data as possible. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use the App.

  • Subscription Status: If you subscribe, Apple or Google handles all payment processing. We only receive a confirmation that your subscription is active — we never see your payment details, email, or Apple/Google ID.
  • Local Storage Only: Your reading progress, bookmarks, notification preferences, and settings are stored entirely on your device. We cannot access this data.
  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels. Your reading habits stay on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

Since we collect minimal data, our use is equally minimal:

  • To verify your subscription status so you can access premium content
  • To deliver local push notifications you have opted into (Daily Briefing, new cases)
  • To maintain the App's functionality and fix crashes

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent any information to third parties. We do not share data with advertisers or analytics companies. The only third party involved is Apple or Google for subscription payment processing — and they handle that entirely on their end.

5. Data Storage & Security

All your data (reading progress, bookmarks, preferences) is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal data on external servers. Your information never leaves your phone unless you choose to delete the app.

6. Your Rights

  • Opt-Out: You can disable notifications at any time through the App settings or your device settings.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data (progress, bookmarks, preferences) from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to external websites (e.g., CIA Reading Room, National Archives). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Content Disclaimer

All content in Deep File is derived from publicly available declassified government documents and information gathered from across the internet. We present this information for educational and entertainment purposes. We make no claims regarding the accuracy or completeness of historical intelligence documents or third-party sources.
